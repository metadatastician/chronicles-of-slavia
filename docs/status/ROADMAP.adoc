= Chronicles of Slavia — Roadmap
:toc:

Ground-truthed 2026-08-07 against the built engine and the design canon. Percentages
are evidence-backed or absent; see `READINESS.adoc` for the component grades and
`DEBT.adoc` for what is knowingly owed.

[IMPORTANT]
====
*The governing rule is `docs/design/08-level1-five-zone-map.md`: do not build Zones
B–E until Zone A holds.* Design work on later zones is permitted and ongoing; engine
and SGS work on them is not scheduled. Nothing in this roadmap overrides that.
====

== Where we are

Zone A — the Border Path — is *built and playable*: the free-roam scene, the
two-heroine paper-doll rig, grove birds answering by their own nature, the unstable
crossing, the Rift's interruption, and a full in-game menu with save/load/continue.
The rules core and the ESM are complete and green. 52 tests pass; none fail.

Under `docs/design/23-level-scope-and-pacing.md`, what is built is *segment A1* of a
Zone A that is intended to be roughly five times its current size.

== Next — awaiting owner rulings, not engineering

These are blocked on decisions, and no amount of implementation unblocks them.

. *Zone B's eight questions.* `docs/design/25-zone-b-decision-sheet.md` puts each in
  front of the owner with options and a recommendation; issue #63 tracks them. Zone B
  cannot be scoped until they are ruled.
. *Chronicle I's tone.* `docs/design/13` says "surreal, dangerous, fast-paced"; the
  Kishōtenketsu blueprint in `22` and the built Zone A both say calm and grounding.
  One ruling, applied at source in `13`/`22`.
. *Co-op vs switch-only.* `13` lists Chronicle II as "Dual-Character Simultaneous";
  `08` and the built engine are switch-only. This one touches the engine — settle it
  before Chronicle II is designed against it.

== Then — the engine substrate A2–A5 and Zone B both need

Two limits block *every* zone larger than one screen. Neither is started, and both are
prerequisites rather than features:

* *A moving camera.* `render.rs` spawns one static `Camera2d`; X is hard-clamped to
  ±620. There is no code path for a level wider than a screen.
* *Rooms in `Session`.* Position is one float along one flat `Vec<Beat>`. `docs/design/23`
  calls for discrete connected rooms, each possibly larger than a screen — which needs
  two levels of position (which room, and where within it).

== Zone A, at full scope (`docs/design/23`)

One continuous level, five internal segments, a save point at each boundary.

[cols="1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Segment | Content

| *A1* | Built today. Gains a closing beat: a marauder appears and Anya disrupts the crossing to repel them — protective disruption, distinct from the Rift's accidental kind.
| *A2* | Animals and plants; functional plants and pattern plants; each girl's signature recharge item (Anya: foraged honey; Donna: made yogurt — wild vs. crafted, matching their natures).
| *A3* | NPCs and clothing as legible identity; the girls obtain an artefact, inert until A5; differential NPC reaction becomes a real puzzle tool.
| *A4* | Puzzle dynamics — the place to finally wire the named ability rosters already written in `docs/characters/the-little-book-of-{anya,donna}.adoc`.
| *A5* | Synthesis, plus asymmetric information and theory of mind — the natural home for the ESM, using the A3 artefact.
|===

== After Zone A

* *Zone B — Fracture Line.* Drafted beat-by-beat (`docs/design/24`); the Rift opens a
  seam along the hole in the co-op grammar, and the girls' loyalty is the lever that
  parts them. Gated on the eight rulings.
* *Zones C, D, E.* Each girl alone on her own shore in a world tilted toward her own
  excess, the other visible across the chasm, to a reunion at the Rift Node Chamber.
* *Chronicle II — The Echo Engine.* Chiasmus/ring composition. Structurally *depends*
  on Chronicle I's route existing first — a C→B→A mirror needs a real A→B→C.
* *Chronicle III — The Two Who Were Needed.* Fichtean curve.

== Explicitly not now

Combat · inventory · multiplayer · the full class system · NPC factions · boss design ·
Level 2 · full animal AI · a production art pipeline.

These are parked, not forgotten; several have design material waiting in
`docs/design/04`, `05`, and `19`.

== Release posture

No release is scheduled. Both crates are `0.0.0` deliberately, `docs/status/READINESS.adoc`
carries the honest component grades, and a version number will follow a decision to
publish rather than announce one.
