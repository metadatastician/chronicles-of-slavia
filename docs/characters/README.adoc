= Character Compendia
:toc:

Fan-style, in-world compendia for the two player characters of
_Chronicles of Slavia_ — **Donna** and **Anya**.

== What these are

* `the-little-book-of-donna.adoc` / `.pdf` — everything the design canon
  establishes about Donna: her backstory, her grammar (she *lowers* taxis —
  settles, calms, steadies), her powers, her costume, her bond with Anya, and
  what the design still leaves open about her.
* `the-little-book-of-anya.adoc` / `.pdf` — the same for Anya, who *raises*
  taxis — stirs, quickens, emboldens.

Each book is written in the register of a devoted fan's character guide (the
kind of thing that later becomes a game wiki page), but every factual claim
is sourced from the actual design canon in `docs/design/`, the Zone A game
data (`engine/slavia-core/data/zone-a.sgs.toml`), the engine source
(`engine/slavia-core/src/{character,animal}.rs`), and the playable prototype
(`prototype/zone-a/index.html`). Nothing is invented; open questions are
flagged as open questions, collected in each book's final chapter, rather
than answered with invented lore.

== Rebuilding the PDFs

Requires Ruby + the `asciidoctor-pdf` gem (`gem install --user-install
asciidoctor-pdf`; Guix/system packages are also fine — see the estate
language policy in the root `CLAUDE.md`, which permits Ruby).

[source,sh]
----
cd docs/characters
asciidoctor-pdf the-little-book-of-donna.adoc
asciidoctor-pdf the-little-book-of-anya.adoc
----

Each book pulls its colour theme from `theme/donna-theme.yml` /
`theme/anya-theme.yml` — Donna's palette follows the Rhodope sukman (dark
earthy reds, near-black, cream); Anya's follows the Hutsul sorochka (vivid
red, black, gold).

== Future: turning this into a wiki

Both books are AsciiDoc, which converts cleanly to MediaWiki/DokuWiki/plain
HTML via `asciidoctor` or `pandoc` — the intent flagged when this compendium
was commissioned. Each book's chapter structure (Profile at a Glance / Who
She Is / Before the Rift / Her Grammar / Her Powers / What She Wears / Two
Lands One Heart / Her Side of Slavia / Quotes & Moments / Fan Corner / Open
Questions) maps directly onto a set of wiki sections or sub-pages, should
this move online later.
