## Project summary

**Chronicles of Slavia** is a mythic 2D puzzle-platform prototype about two girls, Anya and Donna, in a world called Slavia.

Core strapline:

> What do you become when the world breaks?

Core world principle:

> Slavia is not just geography. It is memory, medicine, folklore, and moral choice.

The current prototype is **Level 1, Zone A — Border Path**.

The prototype should remain small and focused.

## Current playable concept

The prototype demonstrates:

1. Anya and Donna start together.
2. The player can switch between them.
3. Anya is quicker and more active.
4. Donna is steadier and can brace/stabilize.
5. Birds respond differently to each girl:
   - Anya stirs them.
   - Donna settles them.
   - The Rift interrupts their natural response.
6. Donna stabilizes a bridge.
7. Anya crosses the bridge.
8. Both characters reach an overlook.
9. The Rift awakens and the prototype ends.

## Core mechanics to preserve

### Character switching

- One active character at a time.
- Inactive character remains visible.
- Tab switches active character.

### Anya

Anya represents Chaos, motion, excitement, courage, and quickening.

Prototype behaviour:
- Moves faster.
- Jumps slightly better.
- Stirs birds and natural animals.

Visual direction:
- Very long dark hair, slightly chaotic.
- Ukrainian-inspired colours: blue/yellow accents, with red embroidery motifs.
- Light, forward-moving silhouette.

### Donna

Donna represents Order, stability, care, settling, and protection.

Prototype behaviour:
- Moves more slowly.
- Stabilizes the bridge.
- Settles birds and natural animals.

Visual direction:
- Very long dark hair in a tidy bun.
- Bulgarian-inspired colours: white/green/red accents.
- Grounded, upright, steady silhouette.

### Living Taxis

Animals are not controlled.

Anya and Donna change the rhythm of the living world around them.

- Anya excites, quickens, stirs, emboldens.
- Donna calms, slows, settles, reassures.
- Natural animals respond according to their own nature.
- Unnatural or corrupted things may fail to respond correctly.

This is not animal control. It is a taxic / orientation principle.

### Bridge metaphor

The bridge is the first playable metaphor of the game:

- Anya wants to cross.
- The bridge is unstable.
- Donna steadies it.
- Anya can cross because Donna has made the path hold.

This is the game in miniature.
