# The Chronicles of Slavia — trilogy structure

Three self-contained arcs, one overarching story about identity, balance, and
the bond between two girls from different cultures. Each has its own tone,
world logic, and level-design philosophy, grounded throughout in the same
platforming mechanics and the same two personalities.

## Chronicle I — The Broken Frontier (also drafted as "The Rift Between
Nations" — same Chronicle, name not yet settled)

**Theme:** worlds merging, cultures colliding, instability.
**Tone:** calm, folkloric, with a paradigm-breaking turn.
**Focus:** Anya's impulsiveness vs. Donna's stability.
**Core question:** *why were they chosen?*

A rift tears open between Ukraine and Bulgaria; landscapes, folklore, memory,
and machinery fuse into one unstable world. They must stabilize the frontier
before it spreads. Ends with the discovery that the Rift isn't random — it's
calling them. Full detail in `14-chronicle-i-rift-between-nations.md`. **This
is the currently-built prototype's Chronicle** — Zone A (Border Path) is its
opening zone.

## Chronicle II — The Echo Engine

**Theme:** ancient technology amplifying human traits.
**Tone:** mechanical, mysterious, puzzle-heavy.
**Focus:** the consequences of their personalities.
**Core question:** *what does the world want from them?*

Deep beneath Eastern Europe, an ancient machine built to amplify human traits
into physical reality awakens and begins testing them — Anya's impulsiveness
opens volatile chambers, Donna's calm opens structured ones.

Level concepts: **Sequential Levels** (Anya runs first, Donna follows through
the consequences), **Echo Imprint Levels** (Anya's actions leave ghost
platforms Donna must use), **Logic Chambers** (Donna, multi-phase puzzles),
**Volatility Chambers** (Anya outruns collapsing machinery), **Dual-Character
Simultaneous** (both operate different layers of the machine at once).

Ends with the truth: the machine is searching for balance, and only the two of
them together can provide it. Sets up Chronicle III's *Unity Shift*
foreshadowing (`11-combined-ability-system.md`).

## Chronicle III — The Two Who Were Needed

**Theme:** myth, spirit, identity, unity.
**Tone:** emotional, symbolic, mythic.
**Focus:** their bond, their growth, their destiny.
**Core question:** *who are they meant to become?*

The guardian spirit that split into chaos and order (`01-world-principle.md`,
"Why the Rift chose both") is revealed as the thing the whole world has been
reflecting. Every level is a test of balance — sometimes chaos is needed,
sometimes order, sometimes both, sometimes one must sacrifice progress for the
other.

Level concepts: **Emotional Levels** (the world reacts to fear, doubt, hope —
`10-emotional-world-reaction.md`), **Unity Trials** (solvable only by rapid
switching), **Mirror Levels** (one girl's mistakes become the other's
obstacles), a **Final Trial** (perfect harmony, switching mid-movement — Unity
Shift, `11`).

Ends with the guardian spirit reunited, balance restored — and a final choice:
return home, or stay as its guardians.

## Where the built prototype sits

Everything built and playable so far (Zone A, `slavia-core`) is the opening of
**Chronicle I** only. Chronicles II and III are pure narrative canon, unbuilt
and unscheduled — recorded so the trilogy's shape isn't lost, not as a build
plan.
