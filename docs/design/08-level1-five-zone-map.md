# Level 1 — The First Tear: five-zone map (outline)

The shape Zone A sits within. **Parked** — nothing past Zone A is built or
scheduled; this records where the currently-built prototype fits in the whole
of Level 1, and checks it for consistency with existing canon. Do not build
Zones B–E until Zone A holds (`00-start-here.md`).

## The five zones

1. **Zone A — Border Path (togetherness).** Calm, folkloric. Forest (Ukrainian
   birch) and mountain foothills (Bulgarian) sides, joined by a bridge. Donna
   stabilizes, Anya crosses. **This is the zone currently built** — matches
   `02-zone-a-design.md` closely (its last beat, "Fracture prelude," is a
   foreshadowing tremor, not the rupture itself).
2. **Zone B — Fracture Line (separation).** The Rift ruptures for real; the
   girls are forced apart. Sequel to Zone A's prelude, not a duplicate of it.
3. **Zone C — Anya's Chaos Zone.** Anya alone: volatile, flickering,
   unstable — the concrete, playable form of "Donna taken → world
   over-agitated" (`06-attunement-and-modifiers.md`, *Separation tilts the
   world*). Donna is visible across the chasm throughout.
4. **Zone D — Donna's Order Zone.** Donna alone: heavy, structured, brittle —
   the playable form of "Anya taken → world over-sedate/lifeless." Anya
   visible across the chasm throughout.
5. **Zone E — Rift Node Chamber (reunion).** Symmetrical chamber, a central
   unstable node. Player **switches** between the girls (confirmed: single
   active character, not simultaneous co-op — matches the built engine).
   Anya destabilizes, Donna stabilizes; the node calms only with both. Closing
   line: *"Two were needed."* — direct echo of `01-world-principle.md`.

## Confirms, not conflicts

- **Single-player, switch-based control** is confirmed as the intended model
  end-to-end (Zone E is explicit about it) — matches `slavia-core`'s one
  active-character `World` model. No change needed to the engine.
- Zones C/D are the first concrete *gameplay* expression of the already-written
  separation/absence mechanic — good sign the lore and the level design are
  pulling in the same direction.
