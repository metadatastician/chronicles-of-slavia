# Touchstones & Positioning

No single game is close (see *adjacent games* below); the DNA is a **fusion**.
What each touchstone contributes — and what to leave behind:

| Touchstone | Take | Leave |
|---|---|---|
| **Hunter × Hunter** (Nen / Zen) | rigorous, orthogonal, **rule-based** systems that *reveal character*; limitation-grants-power; the aura (`06`); moral & psychological depth | shōnen power-escalation, tournament arcs — the "everything becomes an ultimate" trap |
| **Axis Powers Hetalia** | **character-as-personification of a land/culture** — Anya *is* Carpathian Ukraine, Donna *is* Rhodope Bulgaria (terrain affinity, folk dress, taxis of their own ground) | the comedy register — play it mythic and earnest ("two lands, one heart") |
| **11 bit studios** (This War of Mine) | **atmosphere as substance**; moral weight; Central/Eastern-European rootedness that *means* something, not decoration | the war-survival grimness — keep Slavia's gentleness |
| **Ultima VII** | a **living, reactive world** (NPCs with real routines; it feels alive and responds); **moral/ethical weight**; the **mystic/apothecary who diagnoses your leaning and offers a path** (`04`); a **home base** you leave and return to (`05`) | the dense CRPG inventory/interface sim — keep it light |
| **Head Over Heels / Chronology** | switch between two beings/states to solve one shared world | — |
| **Ōkami / Brothers / GRIS / Spiritfarer** | living world that responds to your *presence*; two-being bond; emotional world-reaction; non-combat gentleness | — |

## Thesis

**Systems as expressions of identity — personal *and* cultural — governed by
rigorous rules, never a power ladder.** HxH's Nen is the north star for *depth
without escalation*; Hetalia for *culture worn as character*; 11 bit for
*culture as meaning*. It rhymes with the game's own spine: *you wear the answer
to who you have become* (`01`).

## One-line pitch (for communicating it)

> A folkloric **Brothers-meets-Ōkami** with a **Trine-like switch** between two
> girls — one who stirs, one who stills — and **Nen-like systematic depth**,
> set in a Slavic world that reads who you are.

## Adjacent games, by axis (the neighbourhood)

- Two-being bond / tone: **Brothers: A Tale of Two Sons**.
- Living folkloric world you restore: **Ōkami**.
- Switch-to-solve platformer: **Trine**, **The Lost Vikings**, **Head Over Heels**.
- Emotional world-reaction, painterly 2D: **GRIS**, **Spiritfarer**.
- Folk-herbalism / apothecary: **Strange Horticulture**, **Wytchwood**.
- Slavic-folklore setting done seriously: **Yaga**, **Black Book**, **Röki**.
- Identity you *wear* (nearest cousin to patterns): **Sable** (masks).

## Unclaimed ground (no close comparable — the original core)

- The **stir ↔ still opposition** as the whole mechanic.
- **Embroidery as a social/cultural language** and worn expression of your path.
