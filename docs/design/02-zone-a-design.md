# Level 1, Zone A — Border Path

## Purpose

Zone A introduces Anya, Donna, Slavia, and the core emotional grammar of the game.

## Level flow

1. Forest entrance
2. Bird grove
3. Shrine
4. Stream bridge
5. Ridge path
6. Overlook
7. Fracture prelude

## Core beats

### Forest entrance

The player learns basic movement.

### Bird grove

Anya stirs birds.  
Donna settles birds.

### Shrine

The player reads:

"Two lands, one heart."

### Bridge

Donna stabilizes the bridge.  
Anya crosses.

### Overlook

Both characters reach the overlook.  
The world briefly feels whole.

### Fracture

The Rift awakens.  
The birds stop answering normally.

## Current design rule

Do not add more systems to Zone A until these beats feel clear.
