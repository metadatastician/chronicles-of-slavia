# Emotional World-Reaction Mechanics

Slavia is not a passive backdrop — it is a living reflection of Anya and
Donna's emotional states and their bond. This generalizes effects already
seeded elsewhere in canon (Zone C/D's "platforms collapse faster when she
panics" / "machines jam when she hesitates," `08-level1-five-zone-map.md`;
"Separation tilts the world," `06-attunement-and-modifiers.md`) into one named
system, at three layers.

## 1. Personal — each girl's state, moment to moment

**Anya (chaos).** Stressed/afraid/angry/excited → the world grows volatile:
platforms flicker or collapse faster, Time Flicker destabilizes, hazards
speed up, Dash gates spark unpredictably, chaos crystals pulse harder,
creatures grow more aggressive. Confident/joyful → the reverse: Dash Burst
smooths out, Flicker Step's after-images stabilize, collapsing terrain slows,
volatile zones calm.

**Donna (order).** Anxious/overwhelmed/doubtful → the world grows rigid and
brittle: heavy blocks crack, Reinforce weakens, Anchor Step platforms shrink,
logic machines jam or slow, hazards turn methodical and harder to dodge,
creatures grow defensive rather than aggressive. Secure/focused → the reverse:
Reinforce lasts longer, Anchor Step platforms expand, Heavy Lift smooths,
machines sync, stability zones widen.

## 2. Relational — the bond itself, not just individual mood

The heart of the system: the world reacts to *how they are with each other*.

- **Trust** → harmony: mixed zones stabilize, hazards slow, platforms
  strengthen, Rift Nodes glow brighter, combo actions get small bonuses,
  creatures turn curious instead of hostile. The "you're doing the right
  thing" feedback loop.
- **Conflict (an argument)** → fracture: Slavia visibly splits into chaos and
  order zones, character-switching gets harder, duo actions misfire, emotional
  echo trails distort, puzzles behave unpredictably, Rift Nodes resist
  stabilizing. Real narrative tension, without anyone being physically taken.
- **Guilt / protectiveness (one feels responsible for the other)** → the world
  shifts toward *that girl's* influence — Anya's guilt intensifies chaos zones,
  Donna's protectiveness expands stability zones. Built for rescue levels.
- **Reunion after separation** → bloom: colours brighten, platforms stabilize,
  hazards pause briefly, a temporary **Unity Aura** surrounds them, combined
  abilities get a boost. The emotional payoff beat.

Read alongside `06`'s "Separation tilts the world": physical absence is the
extreme, sustained case; this is the same tilt, continuous, and reachable
through feeling alone, even side by side.

## 3. Narrative events — whole-level reshaping at big story beats

- **Emotional Collapse** — one girl hits a breaking point: terrain collapses,
  time distorts, creatures swarm, the other must rescue her, duo mechanics
  disable temporarily. A major story beat.
- **Emotional Surge** — they overcome a conflict or fear together: new paths
  and hidden platforms open, Rift Nodes weaken, combined abilities unlock new
  variants, the world brightens and stabilizes. A Chronicle turning point.
- **Emotional Echo Storm** — both feel something intense at once: echo trails
  fill the level, switching turns hyper-responsive, chaos and order fuse into
  hybrid mechanics, the world turns surreal and dreamlike, a major revelation
  lands. Reserved for a Chronicle I midpoint or finale.

## Why it's kept, and where it sits

It ties gameplay to story, reinforces both personalities, deepens the bond,
supplies natural difficulty variation, and gives level design an emotional
pacing tool almost for free, since it reuses abilities and zone concepts
already named rather than inventing new ones.

**Not in Zone A.** Zone A ships the base grammar only (raise/lower, natural
animals, one bridge, the Rift's blunt override). This system is canon for
later levels, once mood/relationship state exists to react to at all.
