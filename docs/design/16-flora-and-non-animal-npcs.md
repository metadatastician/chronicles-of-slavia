# Flora & Non-Animal Beings (framework — first pass, to refine)

Plants and non-animal beings hang off the **same spine** as animals — the
taxis duality (stir/loosen ↔ still/settle, `06`), nature-gating (`03`, `07`),
and opposition-as-mechanic — extended onto the *growth* axis and Slavia's
**medicine** and **folklore** pillars (`01`).

## Flora and the taxis gift

Taxis is "the rate and direction of a living thing's motion" — for rooted plants
that is the **rate of growth, opening, and sway**. So the girls affect plants by
the same rule that moves animals:

- **Anya raises it** — quickens growth, bursts buds open, shoots vines out,
  germinates seeds, makes tendrils reach, sets brambles thrashing.
- **Donna lowers it** — stills sway, deepens roots, closes buds, calms and
  **retracts** overgrowth, settles a thrashing plant.
- **Nature-gated** (as animals): natural plants answer; **blighted / Rift-touched**
  plants fail to answer naturally (diagnostic); plants **twisted by an enemy**
  act against their nature.
- **Opposition is the mechanic:** Anya grows a vine to bridge a gap or reach a
  ledge; Donna retracts a thorn-wall to pass. What one does, the other undoes.
  (Reeds for deep water are the first seeded case — `05`.)

## Plants have two ORTHOGONAL aspects (owner's framing)

A plant is described by two independent axes — don't conflate them:

**A. Behaviour (taxis)** — how the girls' gift moves it: Anya grows / opens /
extends it, Donna stills / roots / retracts it (above). The traversal & puzzle
layer (vines, reeds, brambles, blooming platforms).

**B. Harvest-type** — what it yields when gathered:
  - **Food plants** — edible *or* poisonous. **Not** pure healing; they *help*
    (sustenance, minor effects) or *harm* (poison). A lesser, situational tier.
  - **Craft plants** — fibres for the **physical repair** of clothing, the
    protection layer (`17-clothing-repair-and-pattern-weaving.md`).
  - **Pattern plants** — tinctures / dyes / special fibres: the materials for
    **pattern weaving**, the embroidery upgrades — "the proper stuff" (`17`).

Independent: a reed can be both a traversal plant *and* a craft fibre; a bloom
can be *food* and a *dye*.

## Healing is its own axis — yogurt & honey (the pure thing)

The **purest** energy / HP restoration is **yogurt and honey** — the shared
Bulgaria/Ukraine staple, a *fixed, special* restorative. It is deliberately
**NOT** a sprawling craftable recipe tree (that was the wrong turn — no invented
recipes, no folk-histories). Beneath it: **food-plants** (minor/situational
help), and the deeper **herbal / spiritual medicine** of the apothecary
(`04`/`05`, treating spiritual wounds per `01`). Three clear rungs, one axis.

## Folk-symbol / sacred plants

"Folk symbols are living forces" (`01`): a fern-flower (*paparać / папороть*),
rue, periwinkle, the vinok florals — living, ritual, plot-bearing. (Pattern
plants above are the *material* side of this; these are the *symbolic* side.)

## Non-animal NPCs — the spirits (will-ful, not commanded)

Spirits are the **supernatural tier** (`07`): the girls' taxis does **not
command** them. Like supernatural animals, a spirit *feels* the aura and
**always knows who Anya and Donna are**, but engages through **relationship,
deception, and moral choice** — good/evil/neutral and lawful/chaotic revealed
only by behaviour, never a glow. Roster, split by the girls' heritages:

| Origin | Spirits (candidates — owner to confirm) |
|---|---|
| Ukrainian / Carpathian (Anya) | **leshy** (forest lord), **rusalka** (water maiden), **domovyk** (house), fern-flower guardian |
| Bulgarian / Rhodope (Donna) | **samodiva / vila** (forest-mountain nymph), **stopan** (house — the home hearth, `05`), gorski (forest) spirits |
| Shared / Rift | corrupted spirits (`05` factions), a **deep-water presence** at the abyss (rusalka/samodiva) |

## The hierarchy of how taxis lands (unifies everything)

- Ordinary flora & fauna → **full** effect (draw out their nature).
- Domesticated → effect **+ reveals** their true nature.
- Controlled / blighted → **barely** touched; the failure is the diagnostic.
- Spirits (supernatural) → **felt, not commanded** — relationship, not control.
