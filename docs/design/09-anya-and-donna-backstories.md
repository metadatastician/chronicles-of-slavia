# Anya and Donna — backstories

Their abilities are not magic bolted onto a character — they are **emotional
extensions** of who each girl already was before the Tear. Every ability named
here matches the archetypes already in canon (`04-classes-parking-lot.md`).

## Anya — the girl who runs forward

**Core identity.** A Ukrainian girl defined by motion — physical, emotional,
mental. She runs toward things, away from things, through things. She is chaos,
but chaos with heart.

**Emotional flaw.** She avoids stillness, because stillness forces her to face
her fears.

**Hidden strength.** Her courage is instinctive — she leaps before she knows she
can land, and somehow she does.

**Pre-Rift life.** Grew up in a small town near the Carpathians. Her father
repaired old machinery; her mother embroidered vyshyvanka patterns. She
inherited both the mechanical curiosity and the symbolic imagination — and also
a restlessness. Always climbing roofs, racing through forests, jumping fences.
Teachers called her "too quick"; friends called her "too intense." She called it
"just trying to get somewhere" — she didn't know where.

**The moment the Rift chose her.** On the day of the Tear she was running —
literally — from a difficult conversation she didn't want to have. Sprinting
across a bridge, heart pounding. The sky cracked; the embroidery on her shirt
glowed; the ground split beneath her. She didn't fall because she tripped — she
fell because the Rift reached up and grabbed her. It saw her chaos. It needed
her chaos.

**How her past shapes her abilities** — extensions, not magic:
- **Dash Burst** — she's always run from things.
- **Impulse Jump** — she leaps before thinking.
- **Time Flicker** — her mind races faster than the world.
- **Chaos Pulse** — her emotions destabilize environments.
- **Momentum Chains** — she gets stronger the faster she moves.

Her chaos is not destructive — it is **transformative**.

**How her past shapes her bond with Donna.** Anya has always felt
misunderstood. Donna is the first person who doesn't try to slow her down — she
tries to *anchor* her. Anya trusts Donna more than she admits.

## Donna — the girl who holds everything together

**Core identity.** A Bulgarian girl defined by structure — physical, emotional,
intellectual. She builds things, repairs things, organizes things. She is
order, but order with compassion.

**Emotional flaw.** She carries too much responsibility and refuses to ask for
help.

**Hidden strength.** Her calm is not passive — it is powerful. She can
stabilize anything she touches.

**Pre-Rift life.** Grew up in a mountain village near the Rhodopes. Her
grandmother carved kukeri masks; her uncle built wooden bridges. She inherited
both the craftsmanship and the protective instinct. Donna was "the reliable
one" — the one who fixed things, stayed calm, didn't break. But inside, she
felt trapped by the expectation of it: she wanted to explore, to create, to be
more than "the stable one."

**The moment the Rift chose her.** On the day of the Tear she was repairing a
collapsed walkway, and paused at a strange vibration in the wood. The mountains
rumbled; the kukeri carvings glowed; the ground folded like paper. She didn't
fall because she slipped — she fell because the Rift opened beneath her. It saw
her order. It needed her order.

**How her past shapes her abilities** — extensions of her stability:
- **Brace** — she's always held things together.
- **Anchor Step** — she creates stability wherever she stands.
- **Heavy Lift** — she carries burdens, literal and emotional.
- **Reinforce** — she strengthens weak structures.
- **Logic Link** — she sees patterns others miss.

Her order is not rigid — it is **protective**.

## The pairing

Neither girl fell by accident, and neither fell alone — the Rift needed exactly
what each of them already was. See `01-world-principle.md` ("Why the Rift chose
both") for how their two natures are, in-world, one guardian spirit split in two.
