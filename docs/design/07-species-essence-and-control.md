# Species Essence, Domestication, and Control

Extends the diagnostic rule in `03-living-taxis.md`: *if an animal does not
respond naturally, this is meaningful.* This doc defines what an animal's
"nature" actually is, and what breaks it.

## Essence — an animal is, first, its species

Every animal carries the **essence of its kind**: beeness, wolfness, horseness.
Most animals are simply this, neutrally — no plot, no agenda, just what they are.
Anya's/Donna's taxis works on this essence directly: raised or lowered, the
animal does *more or less of what it is already doing*.

## Domesticated — a tamed essence, still true

A domesticated animal (a dog) carries a **tamed version** of a wild essence
(wolfness worn down to dogness) — less exciting, but still its *own* true nature.
Taxis affects it on **two fronts at once**:

- the ordinary raise/lower, as any natural animal, *and*
- a pull **toward its underlying wild nature** — a dog nudged back toward
  wolfishness.

This is a built-in tell: an NPC may find their dog acting oddly *undomesticated*
around the girls, with nothing to see and nothing to explain it — because there
is nothing wrong, the girls are simply near.

## Controlled — not its essence anymore

A **controlled** (possessed) animal has been taken over: it is no longer really
"doggy," only in form. This is different from ordinary domestication or
ownership (a loyal guard dog is not "controlled" in this sense).

- Control is **not always evil-motivated** — something else may simply operate
  this way as a means, not out of malice. **Malintended** control is what evil
  does with it. Either way, from Anya's/Donna's side, it is always a bad thing.
- Because a controlled animal's essence is displaced, taxis barely touches it.
  The girls have only **slight** effect, scaled by:
  - the controller's **power**,
  - **adjacency** (how close the controller is),
  - how **long** the animal has been held,
  - and, on the girls' side, higher **mastery**, closeness together, and full
    aura may buy a little more effect than usual.
- **The tell is behavioural, not visual.** A controlled dog looks like a dog
  (unless deliberately marked/dressed to signal it) — it gives itself away by
  **not acting doggedly**: not answering taxis as its essence should, not
  behaving as its kind would. Players may notice, or may miss it entirely — that
  is intended, not a bug to fix.

## Supernatural — the essence itself, personified

A rare, special class: a supernatural animal is closer to **the personification
of its kind's essence** — a supernatural dog is nearer to *dogness itself* than
to any particular dog. Uncommon by design (not a fleshed-out bestiary system —
more principle than roster), but outsized in **plot** relevance, so full
treatment is deferred.

What is fixed now:

- Each carries a rough **good / evil / neutral** and **lawful / chaotic /
  neutral** character — not a developed stat-block system, just enough to give
  it a nature.
- They **cannot** be domesticated (a supernatural animal domesticating its own
  kind would be absurd) — but they **can become companions**, usually for a
  bounded stretch, until whatever purpose brought them together is fulfilled.
- Taxis **does** affect them, and there is one **immutable** rule: a
  supernatural animal **always knows who Anya and Donna are** the moment it is
  within their aura's range — it may not always know precisely *where*, but that
  it is them is never in doubt.
- They can **deceive** the girls. None of good/evil/lawful/chaotic/neutral shows
  as an obvious tell (no glowing evil, no halo) — alignment is **revealed only
  through behaviour**, sometimes via what others say of them.
- Design principle: the girls default to **innocent until proven guilty** — a
  naivety that is itself part of their character, not a design gap. What a
  player *does* about a given companion is left to the player.
- Ordinary animals do not keep pets — young or friends, yes, pets, no. One
  supernatural animal breaking this rule is an idea reserved for later, and is
  meant to read as **a deliberate tell** when it happens.

## Where this sits

Zone A's grove birds are ordinary and neutral — none of domestication, control,
or the supernatural is in scope yet. This document is canon for later levels
where an NPC's animal, or something met on the road, needs to *be* one of these
categories.
