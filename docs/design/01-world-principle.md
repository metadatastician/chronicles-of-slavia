# Slavia — World Principle

Slavia is not just geography.

It is memory, medicine, folklore, and moral choice.

It is a world where places remember what happened in them, where herbs and remedies treat spiritual wounds as much as bodily ones, where folk symbols are not decoration but living forces, and where every act of courage, care, fear, resentment, trust, or betrayal leaves a mark on the land.

Anya and Donna do not simply travel through Slavia.

They are read by it.

They are healed by it.

They are tested by it.

And, eventually, they learn to heal it in return.

## Why the Rift chose both

Not at random. Slavia is a world built on balance, and Anya and Donna are **two
halves of one guardian spirit**:

- Anya — the spirit's impulse.
- Donna — the spirit's structure.
- Together — the spirit's harmony.

This is the origin of the attunement gift (`06-attunement-and-modifiers.md`,
`07-species-essence-and-control.md`): it is theirs alone because they are not
two gifted girls who happen to complement each other, but one nature split in
two. The Rift tore at them *because* they are the one whole thing capable of
being split. Their backstories (`09-anya-and-donna-backstories.md`) are not
decoration — they are the game's emotional engine, and the guardian spirit
itself is glimpsed later (Zone E, `08-level1-five-zone-map.md`) and gathered
piece by piece (Guardian Spirit Fragment collectibles) before that.

## Identity — you wear the answer to who you become

Slavia's core question is *"what do you become when the world breaks?"*
(`00-start-here.md`). The answer is not spoken — it is **worn**. Three designs
that began apart are one system seen three ways:

- the **path** each girl leans into (her class — `04`);
- the **patterns** she weaves into her dress (the facilities she chooses — `17`);
- and how the **world reads her** for it (welcome, trust, how nature and spirits
  respond — `06`, `16`, `17`).

> The path you choose is what you weave; what you weave is what you wear; what
> you wear is what the world reads. **You wear the answer to who you have become.**

This rests on a three-dimensional model of dress — **biological** (protect ↔
expose), **psychological** (reveal ↔ conceal), **social** (include ↔ exclude) —
detailed in `17`. Its philosophical frame is dialectical, a phenomenological
body–self–other triad, with the social axis Simmelian (belonging vs
distinction).
