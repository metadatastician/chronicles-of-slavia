# Imported Chronicle detail — and what it contradicts

## Provenance — read this first

This material arrived from an external drafting session (Gemini), framed as
**"Act I / Act II / Act III"**. That framing is **not canon and is not adopted
here.** The canon unit is the **Chronicle** (`13-chronicles-trilogy-structure.md`):
three self-contained arcs, each an entire game's worth of scope. "Acts" implied
three movements of one story; these are three *stories*.

The three imported Acts map onto the three canon Chronicles, and the mapping is
evidenced rather than assumed — the imported Act II is literally named for the
**Echo Engine**, and independently reconstructs canon's *Echo Imprint Levels* and
*Volatility Chambers*. That is convergence, not coincidence.

**Everything below is a proposal against existing canon, not a replacement of
it.** Where it conflicts, the conflict is recorded, not resolved. Nothing here is
scheduled; per `00-start-here.md` the built scope remains Zone A.

| Imported framing | Canon Chronicle | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| Act I — The Tremoring Forest (Carpathians, Ukraine) | **I — The Broken Frontier** | **Conflicts** (see below) |
| Act II — The Resonating Peaks (Rhodope, Bulgaria) / Echo Engine | **II — The Echo Engine** | Strong; enriches |
| Act III — The Depths of the Rift (Noumenal Boundary) | **III — The Two Who Were Needed** | Partial; drops the guardian spirit |

## The character axis, as physics

New mechanical grounding for the existing personality split. Compatible with
`06-attunement-and-modifiers.md` — this is the *body* of what canon already
describes as the *temperament*.

| | Anya — Verve / kinetic | Donna — Stability / grounded |
|---|---|---|
| **Physics** | Low damping, high kinetic energy, springy | High damping, high mass, planted |
| **Reaction** | Excites, stirs, agitates (bridges shake, animals panic, kinetic streams trigger) | Stabilizes, anchors, cools (platforms freeze, animals quiet, structures solidify) |
| **Growth arc** | Adolescent volatile energy → must learn self-discipline and internal serenity, or dissolve into **dust** | Rigid childhood security → must learn to yield and flow; static stasis → **fluid crystallization** |

### An implicit spine worth noticing

The imported material, read across all three, quietly assigns each Chronicle a
protagonist whose flaw is on trial:

* **Chronicle I — Donna's trial.** Her "hard-fought victory"; she masters her
  anchor to stop structural collapse, growing stable *like a slow crystal*.
* **Chronicle II — Anya's trial.** Her Verve scales out of control; she must move
  with slow, serene precision or lose her own progress.
* **Chronicle III — the synthesis.** Neither alone; a single adult unit.

Canon (`13`) currently assigns Chronicle I the focus "Anya's impulsiveness vs.
Donna's stability" and II "the consequences of their personalities". The imported
spine is sharper and is a genuine proposal: **each girl gets her own Chronicle to
fail and grow in, before they are asked to be one thing.** *Open question — see
below.*

## Chronicle I — The Broken Frontier

**Imported theme:** the End of Childhood; finding a grip in a collapsing world.

**Folklore/visuals:** rushing torrents, **Mavkas** (willow nymphs), the **Arkan**
(circular dance). The earth physically ruptures.

**Hapkido physics — the core principle.** Redirect linear and circular force
rather than fight it. Anya's sprint triggers landslides; Donna's rigid anchoring
*shears* platforms. Neither brute-forces the world. The solution is transfer:
**Anya pours kinetic energy into Donna, who redirects it** to swing massive
obstacles safely.

This is excellent and it is *already the game*. The bridge metaphor in
`README.adoc` — "Anya wants to cross; the bridge will not hold; Donna steadies
it" — is Hapkido physics stated in miniature. The Arkan being a *circular* dance
and Hapkido being *circular* redirection is a folklore/mechanics rhyme worth
keeping.

Note also that it sharpens a flaw for **both**: Donna's anchoring is not simply
"safe" — rigidly applied, it **shears** things. Canon has plenty on Anya's
recklessness having costs; this is the first clean statement of Donna's stability
having them too.

### Conflicts with canon

1. **Geography and sequencing.** The imported Act I is Ukraine/Carpathian only,
   deferring Bulgaria to Act II. Canon Chronicle I is explicitly *both, fused*:
   "a rift tears open **between Ukraine and Bulgaria**; landscapes, folklore,
   memory, and machinery fuse into one unstable world." The **built** Zone A
   already has both sides — "Forest (Ukrainian birch) and mountain foothills
   (Bulgarian), joined by a bridge" (`08-level1-five-zone-map.md`). The imported
   sequencing contradicts shipped code, not just prose.
2. **Birch vs. pine.** Imported says "lush pine forests"; canon Zone A says
   **Ukrainian birch**. Small, but the birch is doing cultural work.
3. **Theme.** Imported gives Chronicle I "the End of Childhood"; canon gives it
   the core question *"why were they chosen?"*. Not incompatible, but they are
   not the same story.

## Chronicle II — The Echo Engine

**Imported theme:** the Adolescent Fog; the internal storm of self-actualization.

**Folklore/visuals:** ancient limestone peaks, mist-filled caves, acoustic
echoes, **Orphean** folklore, **Kukeri** (bell-clad monster guardians).

**Disproportionate Volatility.** Proximity to the hidden Echo Engine *exaggerates
their flaws asymmetrically*:

* **Donna** suffers only a slight drop in mean speed — still a solid, resistant
  anchor.
* **Anya's** Verve scales *exponentially* out of control. Her speed makes her a
  hazard **to herself**.

**Accelerated Forgetting.** Moving too fast prunes and erases Anya's trail of
physical ghost platforms. She must practise restraint and move with slow, serene
precision to progress.

### Where this fits canon well

Canon Chronicle II already has **Echo Imprint Levels** ("Anya's actions leave
ghost platforms Donna must use") and **Volatility Chambers** ("Anya outruns
collapsing machinery"). The imported material arrives at both independently.
Kukeri and Orpheus are new folklore grounding and conflict with nothing.

The Rhodope/underground tension resolves cleanly: canon puts the Echo Engine
"deep beneath Eastern Europe", the imported material puts the player in the peaks
*above* it and speaks of its *proximity*. Engine beneath, peaks above. Consistent.

### Open question

**Whose ghosts, and for whose benefit?** Canon: Anya's ghost platforms are for
**Donna** to use. Imported: Anya's trail is for **Anya's own** progress, and
punishes her speed. Both are good; they are different mechanics. They can
coexist (a trail Donna can use *and* Anya can burn away), but that is a decision,
not a merge.

## Chronicle III — The Two Who Were Needed

**Imported theme:** Young Adulthood; responsibility, duty, the synthesis of
society.

**Visuals:** shifting geometric abstractions, floating village fragments, starry
voids, raw conceptual forces breaking through reality.

### The aesthetic triad

* **The Beautiful** — symmetrical, harmonious folk-pockets requiring perfect
  coordination.
* **The Uncanny** — familiar childhood items and folklore motifs returning
  distorted, dangerous, physically inverted.
* **The Sublime** — overwhelming cosmic scale that *cannot be physically
  crossed*, forcing the player to use reason to **fold the level boundaries**.

### Fichtean Anstoss — mutual constraint

**Anya cannot move unless Donna establishes a boundary to push off of.** They act
as a single synthesized unit. This is the imported material's strongest idea and
it lands exactly on canon's *Unity Shift* (`11-combined-ability-system.md`) and
*Unity Trials* (`13`). It also works with the switch-based engine: Donna sets the
boundary, the player switches, Anya pushes off it. **No co-op required.**

### The Allegory of Parabasis

The interface breaks. Refugees' dialogue boxes, HUD embroidery lines, and input
prompts **become physical platforms** — Anya jumps on them, Donna anchors them.
The HUD embroidery becoming a ledge is a genuinely beautiful fusion of this
game's specific visual language (`17-clothing-repair-and-pattern-weaving.md`)
with its mechanics. Nothing in canon contradicts it.

### Conflicts with canon

1. **The guardian spirit is missing.** Canon is unambiguous that Chronicle III's
   centre is the guardian spirit *that split into chaos and order*
   (`01-world-principle.md`) being revealed as what the world has been reflecting
   all along, ending in reunion and a final choice. The imported Act III is
   framed philosophically (Kant, Fichte) and never mentions it. The philosophy is
   a fine *lens*; it is not a *replacement* for the reveal the whole trilogy is
   built toward.
2. **"Perfect co-op coordination" — the big one.** See below.

## The unresolved co-op question

The imported material repeatedly assumes **simultaneous two-player coordination**
("perfect co-op coordination", "a single, synthesized adult unit", both acting at
once).

Canon says the opposite, and says it as a *confirmation*:

> Player **switches** between the girls (confirmed: single active character, not
> simultaneous co-op — matches the built engine).
> — `08-level1-five-zone-map.md`

And `slavia-core`'s `World` has exactly one active character. This is shipped.

**But canon is not fully self-consistent here either.** Chronicle II's level
concepts already include **"Dual-Character Simultaneous** (both operate different
layers of the machine at once)" (`13`). So the tension pre-dates the import; the
imported material only sharpens it.

This needs an owner decision, and it is load-bearing for the engine:

* **Switch-only** — Anstoss and most Unity Trials still work (set boundary,
  switch, push off). "Dual-Character Simultaneous" must be rewritten or dropped.
* **Switch + true simultaneity later** — the `World` model changes, and ADR-0002's
  L2 needs two active characters. Cost is real but paid once, and earliest need is
  Chronicle II, which is unbuilt and unscheduled.

Nothing needs deciding now. It must be decided **before** any Chronicle II build
work, and it should not be decided by drift.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Chronicle I geography** — is the frontier fused from the start (canon, and
   built), or does Bulgaria arrive in Chronicle II (imported)? *Canon + shipped
   code currently win by default.*
2. **The per-Chronicle trial spine** — adopt "I is Donna's, II is Anya's, III is
   both"? It is sharper than what `13` currently says.
3. **Ghost platforms** — Donna's tool, Anya's self-punishment, or both?
4. **Chronicle III's centre** — guardian-spirit reveal (canon) with the
   Beautiful/Uncanny/Sublime triad as its *aesthetic*, or the triad as the
   substance? Recommend the former: the philosophy is a strong lens on the reveal
   and a weak substitute for it.
5. **Co-op** — see above. The one that touches the engine.
6. **Birch or pine.**
