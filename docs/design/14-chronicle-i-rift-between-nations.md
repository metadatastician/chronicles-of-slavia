# Chronicle I detail — the Rift, why them, and its level types

Expands `13-chronicles-trilogy-structure.md`'s Chronicle I. Folklore-level
symbolism, not geopolitics — a metaphysical rift, not a political one.

## Why Anya and Donna specifically

1. **They embody imbalance.** Anya is impulsive/fast/chaotic; Donna is
   calm/methodical/structured. The Rift feeds on imbalance, so it pulls in
   whoever embodies it most strongly.
2. **They were both already "on the edge" — personally, not politically.**
   Anya was running from something (fear, guilt, responsibility); Donna was
   stuck in something (expectation, duty, stagnation). Matches their
   individual moments-the-Rift-chose-them exactly
   (`09-anya-and-donna-backstories.md`: Anya running from a hard conversation;
   Donna weighed down by being "the reliable one").
3. **Only those who embody both chaos and order can navigate it.** The Rift is
   made of both forces; one girl alone is not enough — the mechanical basis
   for why the whole game requires both of them (`11`, `13`).

## What the Rift looks like

Ukrainian forest merging with Bulgarian mountains, vyshyvanka patterns glowing
like circuitry, kukeri masks floating like spirits, broken machinery fused with
folklore creatures, time behaving inconsistently, gravity shifting in
emotional zones. The world divides into **Personality Zones** — Anya's
(volatile, fast, collapsing), Donna's (heavy, structured, mechanical), and
**Mixed Zones** (unstable hybrids requiring switching).

## Level types (refined taxonomy, with named examples)

1. **Personality Zones** — pure single-girl zones. Anya's: fast collapsing
   platforms, time-freeze crystals, dash-activated gates, movement-reactive
   enemies, puzzles that punish hesitation. Donna's: heavy blocks,
   reinforcement puzzles, slow-moving hazards, multi-step logic machines,
   puzzles that punish rushing.
2. **Forced Character Choice** — pick one girl; the level is radically
   different depending who. Example: **The Shifting Tower** — with Anya,
   collapsing floors/timed jumps/chases; with Donna, heavy levers/structural
   puzzles/slow rising hazards. Built for replay value and emotional
   storytelling ("Anya panics here" / "Donna feels at home here").
3. **Dual-Character Simultaneous** — free switching, both layers active at
   once. Example: **The Mirror Factory** — Anya on the upper catwalks, Donna
   moving machinery below, actions in one layer affecting the other; the final
   puzzle needs both on linked platforms together.
4. **Dual-Character Sequential** — one goes first, the other follows through
   the consequences. Example: **The Echo Cavern** — Anya races collapsing
   tunnels, leaving echo imprints; Donna later uses them as platforms or
   clues; if Anya rushed too hard, Donna's path gets dangerous. Reinforces that
   personality has consequences, not just style.
5. **Rescue Missions** — one girl trapped, the other frees her. Examples:
   **Anya's Collapse** (Anya stuck in a collapsing memory-zone; Donna
   stabilizes the environment to reach her) and **Donna's Burden** / drafted
   again as **Donna's Trial** (Donna pinned under debris; Anya navigates a
   volatile zone to reach her, then Donna stabilizes the chamber so both
   escape). Works in both directions.
6. **Emotional Levels** — internal conflict made physical. Examples: **Anya's
   Doubt** (platforms flicker when she hesitates; Donna later walks through the
   ghosts of Anya's mistakes) and **Donna's Fear** (heavy blocks crumble if she
   overthinks; Anya later dashes through the debris to reach her). The level
   form of `10-emotional-world-reaction.md`'s personal-reaction layer.

## Chronicle I's own opening level: "The First Tear"

One level type within Chronicle I, not a separate thing: Anya outruns
collapsing terrain, Donna stabilizes falling platforms, the player switches to
escape the initial collapse; they land in the merged Slavia. This is
**Level 1** in existing canon (`00-start-here.md`) — the level whose first zone
(Zone A, Border Path) is the one actually built. Its own zones (A–E,
`08-level1-five-zone-map.md`) are the granular breakdown of this one level's
arc — Zone A is calm/before, Zone B is where the Tear actually opens, Zones
C/D are the Personality Zones alone, Zone E is the reunion. This resolves the
timing/geography questions flagged in `12-core-loop-and-opening-level.md`: the
"early Tear" and "merged landing" belong to Zones B onward, not to Zone A
itself, which stays exactly as built — calm, together, two lands still
visually distinct.

## Chronicle I — three-act arc

- **Act I — The Tear.** Pulled in; discover Slavia; learn the world reacts to
  their personalities.
- **Act II — The Unstable Frontier.** Explore chaos/order zones; the Rift is
  expanding; folklore-machinery creatures appear; they start to sense they
  were chosen.
- **Act III — The First Stabilization.** Reach the Rift Core, stabilize the
  first fracture, learn the Rift is calling them. A voice — the guardian
  spirit — whispers: *"Two were needed."* Sets up Chronicle II.

## Story-integration principle

Each level teaches the player something about the girls; each story beat
unlocks a personality trait; each trait unlocks a mechanic; the world reacts to
their emotional growth. (Restates the core premise already in
`01-world-principle.md` and `13` — no new idea, kept here for the level-design
tie-in.)
