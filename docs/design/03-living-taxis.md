# Living Taxis — Animals and Natural Response

Animals in Slavia are not tools, pets, buttons, or controllable units.

They are living indicators, participants, witnesses, and sometimes helpers.

Anya and Donna do not command animals.
They change the rhythm of the world around them.

Anya tends to excite, quicken, stir, embolden, or agitate natural animals.

Donna tends to calm, slow, settle, quieten, or reassure natural animals.

The animal's own nature determines the result.

## Diagnostic rule

Natural animals respond to Anya and Donna.

If an animal does not respond naturally, this is meaningful.

It may be:

- possessed
- corrupted
- false
- enchanted
- afraid beyond normal fear
- masking another presence
- connected to the Rift

## Early use

In Zone A:

- Anya approaches birds: they stir.
- Donna approaches birds: they settle.
- The Rift awakens: they stop answering normally.

## Later use

Later levels can use animals for:

- sound-cover puzzles
- listening puzzles
- timing puzzles
- corruption detection
- moral choices
- absence mechanics
