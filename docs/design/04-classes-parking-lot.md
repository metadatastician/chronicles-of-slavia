# Classes — Parking Lot

Classes should not be introduced before the base emotional grammar is clear.

They are not jobs.

They are ways the Rift reveals what each girl becomes under pressure.

Possible Anya paths — chaos archetypes, each a different expression of her
impulsive, fast, emotional nature (naming note: also drafted once as
"Sprinter" — treat as the same archetype as Runner until renamed):

- **Runner** (speed) — faster Dash Burst, longer Momentum Chains, platforms
  collapse slower when she is confident. Best for chase levels.
- **Flicker** (time) — stronger Time-Flicker, temporary after-image platforms.
  Best for precision puzzles.
- **Wildheart** (chaos) — Chaos Pulse affects larger areas, volatile zones
  react dramatically. Best for destabilizing environments.

Possible Donna paths — order archetypes, each a different expression of her
calm, strong, structured nature:

- **Pillar** (strength) — stronger Heavy Lift, Brace lasts longer. Best for
  heavy puzzles.
- **Architect** (logic) — faster Logic Link, Reinforce creates larger stable
  zones. Best for multi-phase puzzles.
- **Guardian** (protection) — Anchor Step lasts longer, Ground Pulse
  stabilizes hazards. Best for escort or rescue levels.

All three of a girl's paths are still *her* — same base gift, different flavour
of how it manifests. See `08-level1-five-zone-map.md` for where Dash Burst,
Brace, Heavy Lift, and Reinforce were first named (Zones C/D).

Possible in-world framing:

An apothecary asks:

"What do you become when the world breaks?"

The apothecary does not assign classes.  
She diagnoses a leaning and offers a path.

Do not implement this in Zone A yet.
