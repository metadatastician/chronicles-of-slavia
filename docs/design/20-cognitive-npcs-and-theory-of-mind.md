# Cognitive NPCs — local belief and Theory of Mind

Architecture and rationale: `docs/decisions/0004-hybrid-fsm-esm-mind-body-architecture.adoc`.
Legibility (how the player *reads* any of this): `21-manpu-legibility.md`.

## The principle

**An NPC knows what it has seen, heard, or been told — and nothing else.**

No omniscient global triggers. No "the guard turns hostile because the engine
knows you took the artifact." If the guard is hostile, some event put that belief
in *his* head, and a different guard in the same room may still be wrong about
it, forever.

Ignorance, error, and being deceived stop being scripted set-pieces and become
**systemic consequences of a local database**.

## This is already in the canon

`03-living-taxis.md` calls animals "living indicators, participants,
**witnesses**". A witness is precisely an entity with a private, partial record of
what happened. The Epistemic State Machine is that word made executable.

It also makes the **diagnostic rule** mechanical rather than authored. Canon says:
*if an animal does not respond naturally, this is meaningful — it may be
possessed, corrupted, false, enchanted, or afraid beyond normal fear.* With an
ESM, "does not respond naturally" stops being a bespoke flag per encounter and
becomes a *comparison*: **does this creature's belief state predict the response
its essence (`07-species-essence-and-control.md`) says it should have?** When
those diverge, the player is looking at something wrong. The tell is emergent.

## The shape

Three pieces, deliberately at different tempos (ADR-0004):

* **FSM — the body.** 60 Hz, in Bevy (L3). Physics, pathfinding, patrol, chase,
  cry. Never reasons.
* **ESM — the mind.** In `slavia-core` (L2). A **Global Baseline** of truth plus
  per-entity **Deltas** — the deviations that entity actually acquired. Belief is
  `baseline + own deltas`, full stop.
* **Solver — relational.** microKanren, native Rust, in L2. Runs **only on
  epistemic events** (a visual or acoustic change), never per frame. The FSM
  ticks; the ESM *notices*.

Deterministic by construction: all randomness is injected as a seeded generator,
so the mind is provable headlessly. See ADR-0004.

## The relations

### A. Intent guessing — confidence is just the solution count

NPCs track the player's movement vector and unify it against possible targets.
The **number of solutions the solver returns is the confidence signal** — no
separate confidence heuristic needed, which is the whole reason a relational
engine is the right tool here.

| Solutions | Reading | Guard behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Many** | Ambiguous — could be the USB-stick *or* the secret note | Falls back to a **bottleneck** that covers both |
| **One** | Unified — high confidence | **Aggressively intercepts** |
| **Zero** (`failo`) | Contradiction — a prior assumption just broke | *"Wait — I've been tricked!"* Alert; calls reinforcements |

The zero case is the good one. A contradiction is not an error state; it is the
moment the NPC *discovers it was deceived*, and it is generated by the player
actually deceiving him, not by a scripted reveal.

### B. Instruction loss and memory decay

Instructions given to companion NPCs decay over **distance walked or time
elapsed**. The first instruction survives longest; the tail erodes.

```scheme
;; The imported relation, as specified:
(defrel (retained-instructions StepsRemaining FullList RetainedList)
  (conde
    [(== StepsRemaining 0) (== RetainedList FullList)]
    [(> StepsRemaining 0)
     (fresh (head tail decayed-tail)
       (== FullList (cons head tail))
       (decay-chance StepsRemaining tail decayed-tail)
       (== RetainedList (cons head decayed-tail)))]))
```

Note `decay-chance` is stochastic. In L2 that randomness **must be injected and
seeded** (ADR-0004) or the sacred logic stops being provable.

**This is the same mechanic as Chronicle II's Accelerated Forgetting**
(`19-chronicle-detail-imported.md`): Anya moving too fast prunes her own trail.
Instruction decay over steps and trail decay over speed are one concept —
*memory erodes with motion* — at two scales. They should share an implementation,
not become two systems.

### C. Spatial and semantic ambiguity

NPCs match the player's *directions* against their own immediate surroundings,
not against ground truth.

* **Spatial:** "turn right at the gap" — if several dead-ends look like "a gap",
  the NPC backtracks or gets lost, according to confidence (which is again just
  the solution count).
* **Semantic:** describe it poorly — "crimson flower" vs. "scarlet weed" — and
  the merchant unifies **the wrong physical asset** and fetches it.

The semantic case wants `16-flora-and-non-animal-npcs.md` as its vocabulary:
Slavia's flora typology is exactly the sort of dense, near-synonymous naming space
where honest misunderstanding is possible. Being misunderstood by a merchant who
was *listening carefully* is far better comedy and far better worldbuilding than a
dialogue tree.

### D. The Guilty Conscience trap

If a guard holds `(is-hiding-secret 'hostage)` in his ESM, then **any** player
walking that corridor — even entirely by accident — unifies as
`(intent 'expose-secret)`. He panics, shouts, barricades.

**The AI's paranoia leaks the secret.** Nobody scripted a clue; the guard's own
belief state betrayed him because he could not conceive of an innocent passer-by.
This is the strongest argument in the whole design for doing ToM properly: the
information design *emerges from the simulation*. Players will learn to read
overreaction as a map.

### E. Factional reputation and the rotating sentry — **parked, not built**

Recorded for preservation. **Not** implemented:

* **Global reputation** — actions move standings across classes (Sentry,
  Peasantry, Ruling Class).
* **Asymmetric memory** — if one guard catches you in Chronicle I, *only that
  guard* carries the memory node. Rotate him to a new outpost and he remains the
  sole active threat among neutral colleagues.

Two reasons to park:

1. `00-start-here.md` explicitly parks **"all NPC factions"**.
2. The rotating sentry is *structurally* a two-location mechanic. It cannot be
   demonstrated in one zone, and it is the single best argument for when a second
   location earns its build. It is the natural **trigger for Zone B**, once Zone A
   holds (`08-level1-five-zone-map.md`: "do not build Zones B–E until Zone A
   holds").

Asymmetric memory is, incidentally, free once the ESM exists: it *is* per-entity
deltas. The parked part is factions and rotation, not the epistemics.

## Legibility is not optional

An NPC that reasons invisibly is indistinguishable from an NPC that cheats.

If the player cannot see *why* the guard covered the bottleneck, then a
beautifully-argued relational deduction reads as arbitrary — or worse, as the
game reading their inputs. The Manpu layer (`21-manpu-legibility.md`) is
therefore **not decoration; it is the ESM's user interface**, and the ToM is
wasted without it. This is why the two land together.

## Fail-forward makes it mean something

Per ADR-0005 there is no reload. Being seen is not an inconvenience to undo; it
is a fact that a specific NPC now carries. The persistence of belief and the
absence of rollback are the same design commitment viewed from two sides.

## Built vs. parked

**Built now** (L2, `slavia-core/src/esm/`, against a synthetic cast in
`tests/esm_testbed.rs` — Zone A's five beats untouched):

* microKanren (unify / fresh / conj / disj / run)
* Baseline + per-entity Deltas
* Memory decay (seeded, deterministic)
* Intent classification (ambiguous / unified / contradiction)
* The Guilty Conscience trap (falls out of belief + intent)

**Parked:** factional reputation, the rotating sentry, combat, any NPC in a real
zone.
