# Core Game Loop & the Opening Level ("The First Tear")

An earlier, coarser draft of Level 1's opening than the granular canon in
`02-zone-a-design.md` and `08-level1-five-zone-map.md`. Kept because the core
loop is solid and reusable, and because the opening-level shape is good raw
material — but it has two real tensions with the already-built Zone A, flagged
below rather than silently resolved.

## Core loop (repeats across every level)

Choose/switch character → traverse platforming → use personality-linked
abilities → manipulate the environment → solve puzzles that answer each girl
differently → advance story environmentally → reach a stabilization point
(mini-rift) → unlock new abilities/traits. The opening level must teach the
first four steps clearly before asking for the rest.

## Draft opening-level structure (five sections)

1. **The ordinary world** — a quiet, folkloric border town; Anya and Donna
   together, gently at odds (she wants to run ahead, she wants to plan the
   route). Movement only, no enemies, no puzzles. Anya's rush and Donna's
   steadying already foreshadow the imbalance to come.
2. **The Tear opens** — the sky fractures, vyshyvanka patterns glow, kukeri
   spirits flicker, the ground splits, they fall. An automatic-switch escape:
   Anya outruns collapsing terrain, Donna stabilizes falling platforms. First
   teaching moment for "Anya = speed, Donna = stability, switching matters, the
   world reacts."
3. **Landing in the merged world** — Ukrainian forest fused with Bulgarian
   mountains, gravity unstable, embroidery-patterned platforms. Real tutorial:
   switching, basic abilities, environmental reaction, a first two-part puzzle
   (Anya dashes a gate open; Donna moves a block to stabilize the path; both
   together open the exit).
4. **First stabilization point** — a mini-rift node: Anya destabilizes it,
   Donna stabilizes it, together they seal it. A whisper: *"Two were needed."*
   Sets up the whole trilogy.
5. **Exit and foreshadowing** — the world calms briefly, a distant rumble, a
   guardian-spirit silhouette flickers, the path opens. Pure atmosphere, no
   danger.

## Where this stands relative to built canon

**Resolved** by `14-chronicle-i-rift-between-nations.md`: this draft is Chronicle
I's own opening *level* ("The First Tear," `00-start-here.md`), whose zones
(A–E, `08`) are the granular breakdown of exactly these five sections — Zone A
is calm/before, Zone B is where the Tear actually opens, Zones C/D are the
Personality Zones alone, Zone E is the reunion. The two tensions below were
real, but only because this draft and the built Zone A were describing
different *zones of the same level*, not competing designs of Zone A itself.
Kept for the record of how the resolution was reached.

**The original two tensions (for the record):**

- **Timing of the Tear.** Here it opens *early* (Section 2), right after a
  brief ordinary-world intro. In the built Zone A, the fracture is the *last*
  beat — "Fracture prelude" (`02-zone-a-design.md`) — a foreshadowing tremor,
  not yet the full escape/fall. Those may be two different events at two
  different points in Level 1 (this draft's Tear ≈ the eventual Zone B
  rupture, `08`), or this draft may simply predate the decision to stretch the
  approach-to-the-Rift across a whole zone. Needs an owner call before Level 1
  is built past Zone A.
- **Merged vs. separate geography.** This draft's Section 3 is an already-fused
  hybrid world. Built Zone A keeps the two lands visually distinct — forest
  left, mountains right, joined only by a bridge — because that separateness
  *is* the point ("two lands, one heart"). The fused-hybrid aesthetic may
  belong to a *later* zone (post-Tear proper), not to Zone A's Border Path.

**No tension — the shared image asset.** The concept art (birch forest and
village on the left, mountains on the right, a rift splitting the sky between
them, a glowing gate) matches Zone A's established visual anchors closely.
Usable as-is, no reconciliation needed.

Nothing here changes the built Zone A or `slavia-core`. The core loop is sound
canon regardless of how the tensions resolve.
