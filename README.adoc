== What this is

*Chronicles of Slavia Prototype* is a mythic 2D puzzle-platform prototype about two girls, Anya and Donna, whose bond shapes a world of memory, medicine, folklore, and moral choice.

The central strapline is:

[quote]

---

What do you become when the world breaks?

---

The core world principle is:

[quote]

---

Slavia is not just geography. It is memory, medicine, folklore, and moral choice.

---

The current focus is:

* *Level 1* — The First Tear
* *Zone A* — Border Path

This first playable mock is deliberately small. It is not trying to build the whole game. It exists to test the emotional grammar of the game in its simplest form.

== Current prototype goal

The current Zone A prototype should show that:

* Anya moves, stirs, quickens, and excites.
* Donna steadies, settles, calms, and holds.
* Natural animals answer them differently.
* Donna can stabilize what Anya crosses.
* The Rift interrupts the natural answer of the world.

The prototype succeeds if the player can feel:

[quote]

---

Anya and Donna are different. +
They need each other. +
Slavia itself knows this.

---

== UMS and Enaction status

Universal Modding Studio now has a bounded, validated Zone A / Border Path
profile containing Anya, Donna, birds, the unstable bridge, a shrine, one Rift
disturbance and a cooperative completion condition. It captures quickening,
settling, responses according to the recipient's nature, environmental
receptivity, place memory, moral/world marking, Rift
disruption/inversion/corruption/severance, and the cost of either heroine's
absence.

That profile is evidence that UMS can describe Slavia without making UMS Core
IDApTIK-specific. It is not a Slavia runtime package integration: no loader was
added here, and this game does not depend on the UMS UI.

Enaction is a future source of neutral deterministic primitives—events,
appraisal domains, relationships, influence and memory—not a shared narrative
vocabulary. The terms and rules above remain Slavia-owned. See ADR-0004.

== Core design principles

=== Slavia

Slavia is not only a fantasy location.

It is a world where places remember, remedies matter, folklore acts, and moral decisions leave marks on the land.

Slavia is therefore:

* memory
* medicine
* folklore
* moral choice

=== Anya

Anya is associated with Chaos, movement, courage, excitement, and quickening.

Prototype role:

* faster movement
* slightly more agile
* stirs birds and living things
* represents the force that wakes the world

Visual direction:

* very long dark hair, slightly chaotic
* Ukrainian-inspired blue and yellow accents
* red embroidery motifs
* light, forward-moving silhouette

=== Donna

Donna is associated with Order, care, structure, calm, and settling.

Prototype role:

* slower movement
* stabilises the bridge
* settles birds and living things
* represents the force that lets the world hold

Visual direction:

* very long dark hair in a tidy bun
* Bulgarian-inspired white, green, and red accents
* grounded, upright silhouette
* stronger, steadier posture

=== Living Taxis

Animals in Slavia are not tools, pets, buttons, or controllable units.

Anya and Donna do not command animals. They change the rhythm of the living world around them.

Anya tends to:

* excite
* quicken
* stir
* embolden
* agitate

Donna tends to:

* calm
* slow
* settle
* quieten
* reassure

Natural animals respond according to their own nature.

If an animal does not respond naturally, this is meaningful. It may suggest corruption, possession, enchantment, false life, fear beyond normal fear, or Rift influence.

=== The bridge metaphor

The bridge is the first playable metaphor of the game.

Anya wants to cross. +
The bridge will not hold. +
Donna steadies it. +
Anya crosses because Donna has made the path hold.

This is the game in miniature.

== Development status

This project is at the exploratory playable-prototype stage.

Current focus:

. Keep Zone A small.
. Refine the existing emotional beats.
. Avoid adding major new systems too early.
. Preserve larger ideas in design notes rather than implementing them immediately.

Do not add yet:

* combat
* inventory
* multiplayer
* full class system
* full NPC factions
* full boss design
* Level 2
* full animal AI
* save/load system
* production art pipeline

== Parked design directions

The following ideas are important but not currently implemented.

=== Apothecary and class paths

A future apothecary scene may introduce class-like paths through questions of virtue, medicine, and moral diagnosis.

Possible Anya paths:

* Runner
* Flicker
* Wildheart

Possible Donna paths:

* Pillar
* Architect
* Guardian

The apothecary's central question may be:

[quote]

---

What do you become when the world breaks?

---

=== Absence mechanic

If Anya is absent, the world becomes over-settled, muted, heavy, slow, and sedated.

If Donna is absent, the world becomes over-stirred, noisy, unstable, fast, and turbulent.

This should eventually make the player feel the cost of separation not only through missing abilities, but through the whole changed behaviour of Slavia.

=== Mirror of Misalignment

A future corruption boss may weaponise Anya and Donna's flaws against each other.

This is currently parked and should not be implemented in Zone A.
